The exposition is too cursory throughout.  Much of the notation is undefined.  For example, the symbols f_j, \beta_j, p_k, and X_j, Y, y, and n, are not defined. What is the range of the index j?  One may, relying on conventions, guess what these symbols represent, but they are never defined.

No expression is given for the MDI (this acronym is not defined in the body of the manuscript). Nor is an expression given for impurity importance.

The writing is quite strange.  For example, Section 5 begins with “Data: y = a …”. This is being too terse, and likely is an artifact of the author’s having used AI in the preparation of the manuscript (the author declares this, for which the AE is grateful).  

The sub-section numberings are bizarre. In addition, phrases are used like “high-cardinality features” which are not explained. There are strange sentences like “shallow trees starve weak features.”  This sentence (which seems typical of an AI-generated sentence) is very imprecise. 

While it may be interesting to investigate equivocal use of MDI as a variable importance measure, this work does not appear to approach the problem carefully enough.
